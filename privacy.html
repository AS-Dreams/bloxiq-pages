Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bloxiq Block Puzzle handles information. Bloxiq Block Puzzle is a mobile puzzle game developed by AS-Dreams.

Data Collection

Bloxiq Block Puzzle does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

Local Game Data

The app may save game progress, scores, settings, and similar gameplay information locally on your device. This information is used only to run the game experience on your device and is not sent to AS-Dreams servers.

Tracking and Advertising

Bloxiq Block Puzzle may show ads through Google AdMob. Ads are intended to be non-personalized or contextual in the first advertising-supported release. The app does not use App Tracking Transparency permission unless a future version enables personalized advertising or other tracking features.

Google Mobile Ads may process advertising-related data to deliver, limit, measure, and protect ads. Google User Messaging Platform may be used where consent choices are required. Bloxiq Block Puzzle does not sell personal information and does not use third-party analytics in this release plan.

Children's Privacy

Bloxiq Block Puzzle does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
