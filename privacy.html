Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bloxiq Block Puzzle handles information. Bloxiq Block Puzzle is a mobile puzzle game developed by AS-Dreams.

Data Collection

The current App Store version of Bloxiq Block Puzzle does not collect personal data. The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

Local Game Data

The app may save game progress, scores, settings, and similar gameplay information locally on your device. This information is used only to run the game experience on your device and is not sent to AS-Dreams servers.

Tracking and Advertising

The current App Store version of Bloxiq Block Puzzle does not track users across apps or websites and does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. If a future version adds ads, analytics, online services, or in-app purchases, this Privacy Policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before that version is released.

Children's Privacy

Bloxiq Block Puzzle does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

For help with Bloxiq Block Puzzle, please visit the support page.
